# VFS2024

### Verification for Collaboration: Strategic Partnership with Hitachi Energy

{% include figure.html image="images/projects/vfs2024.jpg" caption="Tap changer illustration" width="400px" %}

**Duration:** 1 September 2024 – 31 August 2025  
**Funder:** Uppsala Innovation Centre  
**Partners:** XRIA Lab and Hitachi Energy  
**Status:** Completed

The project addressed the challenge of improving the training, service, and marketing of tap changers, critical components in transformers with intricate mechanical parts and complex thermal behavior, resulting in multiphysics interactions.
Their complexity makes tap changers difficult to understand, maintain, and market effectively, leading to inefficiencies and higher costs.
Given the importance of transformers in electricity transmission, the collaboration contributes to modernizing electricity transfer technology and supports the green transition.
