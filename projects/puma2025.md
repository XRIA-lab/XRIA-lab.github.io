# PUMA2025

### Educating Machine Design with Extended Reality

**Duration:** 1 September 2025 – 31 August 2026  
**Funder:** Uppsala University  
**Project funding:** [SEK 238,000](https://www.uu.se/en/staff/news/2025/2025-06-27-new-educational-development-funding-also-for-distinguished-university-teachers)  
**Partners:** XRIA Lab and the Division of Applied Mechanics  
**Status:** Completed

This project developed an XR learning environment for teaching machine design in the Mechanical Engineering program at Uppsala University.
Using XR as a virtual lab, students explore and interact with complex mechanical components such as shafts, bearings, and gears in realistic 3D contexts.
The project aimed to reduce cognitive load, improve understanding of design principles, and increase student engagement.
