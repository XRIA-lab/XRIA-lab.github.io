# ARTISAN

### Agentic Reality Training in Skill Acquisition for Next-Generation Manufacturing

{% include figure.html image="images/projects/artisan.jpg" caption="ARTISAN agentic system flow" width="700px" %}

**Duration:** 1 September 2025 – 31 August 2028  
**Funder:** Sweden's Innovation Agency (Vinnova)  
**Vinnova funding:** [SEK 5,999,868](https://www.vinnova.se/en/p/artisan-agentic-reality-training-in-skill-acquisition-for-next-generation-manufacturing/)  
**Partners:** AugmentedRealm, Hitachi Energy, Solme, Ekets Group, and Uppsala University (XRIA Lab)  
**Status:** Ongoing

ARTISAN captures expert knowledge and delivers real-time, immersive guidance to new operators through XR headsets.
Combining AI-based process analysis, multimodal data capture, and contextual XR interfaces, the system helps operators learn complex tasks directly on the shop floor without requiring direct supervision.
Key capabilities include voice-guided instructions, object recognition, and adaptive step-by-step support based on user actions and task context.

The goal is to accelerate training, reduce errors, and retain critical expertise in an increasingly automated industrial landscape.
Designed for flexibility and scalability, ARTISAN offers a practical solution for improving workforce readiness across a range of manufacturing environments, and contributes to the digital transformation of Swedish industry.
