Thomas Schmitt is an industrial doctoral researcher employed by Scania in collaboration with Uppsala University, pursuing a Ph.D. on energy efficiency in production systems.
He received his M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering and Management from Uppsala University.
His research covers the economic and environmental performance analysis of production systems, particularly through simulation and data-driven modeling.
