Matías Urenda Moris is an Associate Professor at Uppsala University.
He holds a B.Sc. in Automation Engineering from the University of Skövde, an M.Sc. in Manufacturing Management from Loughborough University, and a Ph.D. in Healthcare Engineering from De Montfort University.
His main research area is discrete-event simulation for manufacturing and healthcare systems, with an emphasis on system modeling and analysis.
