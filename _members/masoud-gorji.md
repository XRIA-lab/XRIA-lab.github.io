Masoud Rahimi Gorji is a PhD student at XRIA Lab.
He received his M.Sc. in Mechatronics from Amirkabir University of Technology.
His research focuses on multimodal AI and XR for manufacturing skill transfer, with interests in human-robot interaction, computer vision, robotics, and adaptive XR guidance systems for industrial training.
