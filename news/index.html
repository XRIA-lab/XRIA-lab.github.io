MANUFACTOR kicks off, our first Horizon Europe project

May 01, 2026
projects funding horizon-europe

The Horizon Europe project MANUFACTOR starts today and runs until April 2029. XRIA Lab joins 13 partners across Europe to build a multi-agent platform that combines XR, multi-agent AI, human digital twins, and multimodal sensing for cognitive and physical augmentation of industrial operators and managers.
