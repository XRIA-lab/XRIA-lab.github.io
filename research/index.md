# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-vr-cardboard" %}Research

Industry is moving toward human-centered, resilient, and sustainable production (Industry 5.0).
XRIA Lab studies how extended reality and artificial intelligence can put people at the center of that transition: helping operators acquire skills, giving managers explainable decision support, and making production systems more energy-efficient.
Our work is organized around three connected themes.

{% include section.html %}

## XR & Human Augmentation

{% include figure.html image="images/research/xr-machine-interaction.jpg" caption="XR-based real-time machine interaction in a learning factory" width="800px" %}

We develop XR systems that connect operators to live machine data, deliver adaptive step-by-step guidance, and capture expert knowledge so it can be transferred to new staff.
Current work combines XR headsets with multimodal sensing, computer vision, and AI agents that assess workload, posture, and task progress.

**Projects:** [MANUFACTOR]({{ "projects/manufactor" | relative_url }}), [ARTISAN]({{ "projects/artisan" | relative_url }}), [VFS2024]({{ "projects/vfs2024" | relative_url }})

## AI Decision Support & Simulation-Based Optimization

{% include figure.html image="images/projects/ai-compete.jpg" caption="AI-COMPETE: coordinated multi-agent decision support" width="800px" %}

We use discrete-event simulation, simulation-based optimization with knowledge discovery, reinforcement learning, and large language models to support production planning and energy efficiency.
Examples include aligning production schedules with renewable energy availability, identifying and valuing industrial energy flexibility, and generating and adapting simulation models automatically with generative AI.

**Projects:** [AI-COMPETE]({{ "projects/ai-compete" | relative_url }}), [FLEXIVA]({{ "projects/flexiva" | relative_url }}) (from 2027), [MANUFACTOR]({{ "projects/manufactor" | relative_url }})

## XR in Engineering Education

We design and evaluate XR virtual labs for engineering courses, such as HoloMech for solid mechanics and an XR environment for machine design, and study their effect on student motivation, spatial reasoning, and cognitive load.

**Projects:** [PUMA2023 – HoloMech]({{ "projects/puma2023" | relative_url }}), [PUMA2025]({{ "projects/puma2025" | relative_url }}) · see also our [courses]({{ "teaching" | relative_url }})
