Master’s thesis projects

We supervise Master’s theses in industrial engineering, XR, AI, and optimization, often in collaboration with our industrial partners. Example directions, connected to our current projects:

  • XR guidance and knowledge capture for operator training (ARTISAN)
  • Multi-agent and reinforcement-learning decision support for energy-aware production planning (AI-COMPETE)
  • Multimodal sensing of operator workload and ergonomics with XR feedback (MANUFACTOR)
  • Simulation-based optimization and generative-AI support for discrete-event simulation
  • Identifying and valuing energy flexibility in production from EMS and production data (FLEXIVA)
  • XR virtual labs for engineering education
