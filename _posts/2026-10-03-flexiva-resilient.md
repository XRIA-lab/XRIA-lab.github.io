Together with Scania and the [USER – Uppsala Smart Energy Research group](https://www.uu.se/en/research/user-uppsala-smart-energy-research-group), XRIA Lab is joining the extension of the [RESILIENT Competence Centre for Resilient Energy Systems](https://sites.mdu.se/resilient/en/resilient) for 2027–2031.

Our project, [FLEXIVA]({{ "projects/flexiva" | relative_url }}), tackles the barriers to industrial energy flexibility: where the flexibility potential lies in production, what it takes to provide it within operational constraints, and when it makes economic sense.
FLEXIVA will recruit a postdoctoral researcher; see [Join Us]({{ "join" | relative_url }}).
