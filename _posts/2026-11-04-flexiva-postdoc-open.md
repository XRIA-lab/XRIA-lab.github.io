XRIA Lab is recruiting a **postdoctoral researcher in Industrial Engineering and Management, specializing in energy flexibility in industrial operations**.
The two-year, full-time position is part of the [FLEXIVA]({{ "projects/flexiva" | relative_url }}) project in the RESILIENT competence centre, co-financed by the Swedish Energy Agency, and is carried out in close collaboration with Scania.

You will study how manufacturing companies can adapt when and how they use electricity to support the energy system while maintaining productivity, using approaches such as data analytics, simulation and digital twins, optimization, and AI-based decision support.

**Application deadline: 27 November 2026** (ref. UFV-PA 2026/3382). More details are on our [Join Us]({{ "join" | relative_url }}) page.
