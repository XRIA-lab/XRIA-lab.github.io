XRIA Lab starts two new three-year projects funded by Sweden's Innovation Agency (Vinnova).

[ARTISAN]({{ "projects/artisan" | relative_url }}) develops agentic XR training that captures expert knowledge and guides new operators on the shop floor, together with AugmentedRealm, Hitachi Energy, Solme, and Ekets Group.

[AI-COMPETE]({{ "projects/ai-compete" | relative_url }}) develops a multi-agent AI decision-support system that aligns production planning with renewable energy availability, together with the University of Skövde, Volvo Penta, Scania, Daloc AB, and Evoma AB.

At the same time, the education project [PUMA2025]({{ "projects/puma2025" | relative_url }}) starts, bringing XR into machine design teaching at Uppsala University.
