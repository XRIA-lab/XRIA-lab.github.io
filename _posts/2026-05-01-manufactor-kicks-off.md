The Horizon Europe project [MANUFACTOR]({{ "projects/manufactor" | relative_url }}) starts today and runs until April 2029.
XRIA Lab joins 13 partners across Europe to build a multi-agent platform that combines XR, multi-agent AI, human digital twins, and multimodal sensing for cognitive and physical augmentation of industrial operators and managers.
