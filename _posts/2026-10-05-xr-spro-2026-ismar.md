Kaveh Amouzgar is co-organizing the **[XR-SPro 2026 workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/xr-spro2026/program?authuser=0)** on extended reality for smart production, held on **6 October 2026** as part of **[IEEE ISMAR 2026](https://www.ieeeismar.net/2026/)** in Bari, Italy.

XRIA Lab contributes two workshop papers to the program:

**From XR-Captured Demonstrations to Evidence-Grounded Operator Assistance: A Reasoning Layer for Human–AI Symbiosis**  
Kaveh Amouzgar, Eric Dario Márquez León, Masoud Rahimigorji, and Cedric Kaneza  
_Paper Session 1: AI-Enhanced XR Systems and Human–AI Assistance in Smart Production_

An expert's narrated assembly demonstration, recorded with an XR headset, is turned into an enriched procedural knowledge graph with inferred requirements, validation status, and provenance. This graph grounds an LLM assistant that answers novice operators' questions, so its guidance can be traced back to evidence. The work is part of the [ARTISAN]({{ "projects/artisan" | relative_url }}) project.

**A Non-Intrusive XR Interaction Stack for Monitoring, Programming, and Simulating Collaborative Robots in Smart Production**  
Kaveh Amouzgar, Simon Arnestrand, Oscar Rosengren, Elis Amcoff, and Erik Hultman  
_Paper Session 2: XR Interaction, Robot Collaboration, and Immersive Training, plus the hands-on demo session_

A HoloLens 2 application connects to a UR3 collaborative robot in an existing learning-factory production cell without modifying its PLC or robot logic. Operators can position the robot with a holographic target box, author multi-step programs, monitor joint temperatures and motion trails, test programs on a digital twin, and export them as URScript.
